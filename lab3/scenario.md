# Lab 3 scenario — Fah's shop

Fah runs a small clothing shop on Instagram and LINE from her dorm. Every
evening brings forty to sixty messages: orders, questions about sizes and
shipping, the occasional angry customer, and spam. Urgent complaints get buried
under "do you ship to Chiang Mai?". She wants one place to paste a message and
instantly see what kind it is, how urgent it is, what it says in one line, and
a reply she can edit and send.
